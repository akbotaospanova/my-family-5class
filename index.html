🪐 Grammar Box

Тәуелдік 's, Whose және Who's
Ex. 2 · p. 33

Possessive 's

Owner+'s+noun

Sam's daughter = the daughter of Sam

Kate and Claire's dad = one dad for two girls

Kazakh: Самның қызы · Russian: дочь Сэма

Whose…? 🔑

Whose= чей? кімнің?

Whose book is this? — It's Paula's.

Whose dad is Sam? — He's Kate's dad.

Who's…? 🙋

Who's= Who is

Who's John? — He's Sue's brother.

⚠️ who's sounds like whose, but means “who is”.

🏠 Homework

Деңгейіңді таңда (A, B немесе C)
Level A

My Family Tree

  • Draw your family tree (or use a photo).
  • Label at least 8 family members in English.
  • Write the names under the pictures.
Example: Nurlan — grandfather, Saule — grandmother…
✔ 8 words · correct spelling · neat work
Level B

About My Family

  • Write 5–6 sentences about your family.
  • Use 's in every sentence.
  • Use target words: uncle, aunt, cousin, niece, nephew…
Example: Erlan is my dad's brother. He's my uncle. Aru is my uncle's daughter. She's my cousin.
✔ 5–6 sentences · 's used correctly · 6+ family words
Level C

Meet My Family

  • Write a dialogue with 4 questions: Who's…? / Whose…?
  • or record a 1-minute audio “Meet my family”.
  • Be ready to present it next lesson.
A: Who's Gulnar? B: She's my aunt. A: Whose wife is she? B: She's my uncle Erlan's wife.
✔ 4 correct questions · clear pronunciation · good answers
📜

Values task · Жеті ата (Seven ancestors)

Ask your grandparents about your seven ancestors. Write their names in English letters: “My father's father is…”. Knowing your roots is part of being an Adal Azamat.
